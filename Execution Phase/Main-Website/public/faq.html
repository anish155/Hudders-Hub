Common Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering fresh food locally.

We connect you directly with local market traders (butchers, greengrocers, etc.). Simply browse our marketplace, add fresh items to your cart, choose a collection time, and pick up your order from the stall. It's like shopping at the market, but without the queues.

Currently, we offer Click & Collect from our market stalls. When you checkout, you can select a specific date and time slot that suits you. This ensures your food is picked fresh and ready for you.

Yes, absolutely. We use industry-standard encryption to protect your payment details. You pay securely online at checkout, and you don't need to exchange cash or cards at the stall.

Since our produce is sourced fresh for you, cancellations must be made at least 24 hours before your collection slot. If you need to change your slot time, please contact our support team via the Contact page.

We are based in Huddersfield, West Yorkshire. All our products are sourced from local traders within the region to ensure peak freshness and support the community.
